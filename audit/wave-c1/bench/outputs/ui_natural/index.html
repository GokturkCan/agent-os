FAQ

Questions

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Files are encrypted on your device before upload (AES-256) and stay encrypted at rest.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from the billing page; your backups stay readable for 30 days after.

Do you support Linux?

macOS and Windows today. A Linux agent is planned for later this year.
