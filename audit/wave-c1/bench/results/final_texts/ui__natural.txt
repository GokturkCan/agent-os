I wrote `redesigned/index.html` but didn't open it in a browser or run any checks. The layout, contrast and keyboard behavior are from reading the code, not from testing.
- **Look:** The page is built around a meridian-line motif. It has a deep-ink hero with thin amber longitude arcs, a warm paper background, a Georgia display face, and a system sans for body text. The Team plan card is inverted dark. It's one file with inline CSS/JS, no external requests, and the plan icons are inline SVG.
- **Content:** All plan names, prices and features, the three FAQ questions and answers, the trial CTA, the subscribe form and the footer links are kept. The wording changes are a "Pick the plan that fits your team" heading, an "Email address" label and some small button text. The "Features" and "Pricing" nav links both point to the plans section, because the original had no separate features section. The trial button, plan buttons and subscribe form show a short confirmation message, since the originals were empty no-op functions.
- **Responsive and accessibility:** The layout uses an auto-fit grid and fluid type, and it avoids horizontal scrolling by design from 360px up to 1600px. It has a skip link, header, main and footer landmarks, one `h1` with `h2`/`h3` headings, and a labelled email field with an error message that screen readers announce. The FAQ uses native `<details>`/`<summary>`, so Tab, Enter and Space work. Focus rings are 3px, controls are at least 44px tall, and the text colors were picked for AA contrast.
